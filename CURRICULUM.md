# Master Curriculum Architecture (Levels 0 through 5)

**Pedagogical Alignment**: SEBI Investor Education, NSE NCFM/NISM, CME Group Institute, IBKR Traders' Academy, Zerodha Varsity

  * Concept 0.1.3: Nominal Returns vs Real Returns
  * Concept 0.1.4: Opportunity Cost & Time Value of Money (TVM)
* **Course 0.2: Personal Finance & Asset Allocation**
  * Concept 0.2.1: Income, Expenses, Cash Flow, Assets & Liabilities
  * Concept 0.2.2: Emergency Funds, Debt Repayment & Credit Scores
  * Concept 0.2.3: Risk Tolerance, Risk Capacity & Investment Horizon
  * Concept 0.2.4: Compounding Frequency & The Rule of 72
* **Course 0.3: Mathematics & Quantitative Foundations**
  * Concept 0.3.1: Percentages, Basis Points (bps) & Percentage Changes
  * Concept 0.3.2: Simple Interest vs Compound Interest Formulas
  * Concept 0.3.3: CAGR (Compound Annual Growth Rate) & Annualized Returns
  * Concept 0.3.4: Probability, Expected Value ($E$), Variance & Standard Deviation ($\sigma$)

* **Course 1.3: Order Types & Execution Rules**
  * Concept 1.3.1: Market Orders, Limit Orders & Fill Uncertainty
