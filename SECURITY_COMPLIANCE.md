# Regulatory Compliance & Legal Boundaries
## Project: MarketMaster Academy
**Jurisdictions**: India (SEBI, RBI, DPDP Act 2023) & International (GDPR, FTC)  
**Version**: 1.0.0  

---

### 1. SEBI Regulatory Boundary Disclaimers
* MarketMaster Academy is an educational research and simulation tool.
* It is **NOT** a registered stockbroker, portfolio manager, or research analyst under SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013 or SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations, 2014.
* **No Real Money Transactions**: Virtual currency used in the simulator has zero monetary value and cannot be redeemed, withdrawn, or transferred.
* **No Investment Advice**: Educational content does not constitute financial advice, tip services, or recommendations to buy/sell securities.

---

### 2. Retail Algorithmic Trading Framework Alignment (SEBI Circulars)
* Educates users on exchange algo submission guidelines, kill-switch requirements, order-to-trade ratio (OTR) penalties, and testing standards without claiming to be an approved live algo execution gateway.

---

### 3. Data Protection & Privacy Rights
* **India DPDP Act 2023 Compliance**: Data minimization, clear notice and consent mechanisms, rights of data principal to access, correct, and completely erase personal records.
* **GDPR Compliance**: Right to be Forgotten (`POST /api/v1/auth/delete-account` executes automated cascade deletion of all user records across all tables).
