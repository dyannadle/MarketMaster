01. INSTITUTIONAL DEFINITION menu_book

Delta measures the expected change in option premium per ₹1.00 move in the underlying asset price. Simultaneously, Delta serves market makers as the synthetic hedge ratio and an approximate risk-neutral probability of finishing in-the-money (ITM).

Call Delta: 0.00 to +1.00 Put Delta: -1.00 to 0.00
02. INTUITIVE ANALOG speed

Think of Delta as the Speedometer Transmission Ratio: At ATM (0.50 Δ), your contract captures half the underlying vehicle's velocity. Deep ITM (0.95 Δ) shifts into 1:1 parity mimicking pure shares, while OTM (0.10 Δ) crawls sluggishly.

ATM Sensitivity: ~50% Pass-through
03. MATHEMATICAL DERIVATION functions
Δ = ∂V / ∂S = N(d₁)

Where V is option price, S is asset spot price, and N(d₁) is the cumulative standard normal distribution from Black-Scholes-Merton.

Source: BSM Partial Differential Equation (1973)
