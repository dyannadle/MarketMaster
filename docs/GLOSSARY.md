# Financial & Technical Glossary
## Project: MarketMaster Academy
**Terms**: Over 100+ Essential Financial & Technical Definitions  

* **Ask Price**: The lowest price a seller is willing to accept for a security.
* **Basis**: The difference between the spot price of an asset and the futures price ($F_t - S_t$).
* **Bid Price**: The highest price a buyer is willing to pay for a security.
* **Bid-Ask Spread**: The gap between best ask and best bid ($P_{\text{ask}} - P_{\text{bid}}$).
* **Candlestick**: A chart representation showing Open, High, Low, and Close (OHLC) over a specific time window.
* **Circuit Breaker**: An automatic mechanism halting exchange trading during severe market-wide index plunges.
* **Contango**: Market condition where futures prices trade higher than spot prices.
* **Demat Account**: An electronic depository account holding securities in dematerialized form.
* **Delta ($\Delta$)**: The rate of change of option price with respect to changes in the underlying asset's price.
* **EBITDA**: Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation, and Amortization.
* **Gamma ($\Gamma$)**: The rate of change of Delta with respect to underlying asset price.
* **Iceberg Order**: An execution order sliced into visible display lots to hide true total size.
* **Implied Volatility (IV)**: The market's expectation of future volatility implied by option market prices.
* **Mark-to-Market (MTM)**: Daily or real-time settlement of open positions based on prevailing closing prices.
* **PE Ratio**: Price-to-Earnings ratio (Market Price per share / Earnings Per Share).
* **POV**: Percentage of Volume algorithmic execution strategy.
* **Sharpe Ratio**: Risk-adjusted excess return per unit of total portfolio volatility.
* **Slippage**: The difference between expected execution price and actual fill price.
* **SPAN Margin**: Standard Portfolio Analysis of Risk margin system utilized by Indian exchanges.
* **Theta ($\Theta$)**: The rate of option price decay as expiration approaches (time decay).
* **TWAP**: Time-Weighted Average Price algorithmic execution order.
* **Vega ($\nu$)**: The sensitivity of option price to changes in implied volatility.
* **VWAP**: Volume-Weighted Average Price execution benchmark.
