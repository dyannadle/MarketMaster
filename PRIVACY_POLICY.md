# Privacy Policy
## Project: MarketMaster Academy
**Effective Date**: November 3, 2026  
**Last Updated**: November 3, 2026  
**Compliance Standards**: Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 (India), General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) (EU), Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)  

---

### 1. Data Minimization Commitment
MarketMaster Academy operates on a strict **Data Minimization** principle. We only collect the bare minimum information required to operate your educational account and track your simulation learning progress:
* **Account Credentials**: Email address and cryptographically hashed password.
* **Display Identity**: User-chosen display name or handle (no legal names required).
* **Learning Progress**: Completed courses, quiz scores, skill mastery ratings, and trading journal notes.
* **Virtual Trade Records**: Simulated orders, fills, and virtual account balances.

**What We NEVER Collect**:
* We do **NOT** collect bank account numbers, credit/debit card details, PAN card, Aadhaar numbers, phone numbers, physical residential addresses, or contacts.
* We do **NOT** sell, rent, monetize, or broker personal data to advertisers, brokers, or data brokers.

---

### 2. Age Consent & Children’s Privacy
* MarketMaster Academy is designed for users aged 18 and older.
* **Minors (Under 18)**: Minors may access the educational platform strictly under the supervision and verified consent of a parent or legal guardian.
* We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal data from children under the age of 13. If we become aware that personal data of a minor under 13 has been collected without verifiable parental consent, we immediately delete that account and all associated data.

---

### 3. Third-Party SDK Audit & Tracking Policy
* We strictly audit every software development kit (SDK) used in our web and mobile applications.
* We ban invasive third-party ad networks, telemetry trackers, and social media fingerprinting pixels.
* Privacy-preserving analytics (e.g. self-hosted PostHog) operate with IP anonymization enabled.

---

### 4. Right to Erasure & Data Deletion Requests
Under the DPDP Act 2023 and GDPR, you possess an unconditional **Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten)**:
* **Self-Service Instant Deletion**: Navigate to `Settings` $\to$ `Privacy & Security` $\to$ `Delete Account`. Confirming this action immediately and irreversibly cascades deletion across our database tables (Users, Virtual Accounts, Orders, Positions, Journals).
* **Manual Request**: You may email `privacy@marketmaster.academy` with the subject line *"Data Deletion Request"*. All records will be expunged within 48 business hours with written confirmation.

---

### 5. Email Communications & Unsubscribe Policy
* We only send transactional emails (e.g., password reset, security login notifications).
* Any optional educational digests include a visible, single-click **Unsubscribe link** at the footer. We honor unsubscribe requests instantaneously.

---

### 6. Data Controller & Grievance Officer Contact
In accordance with India DPDP Act 2023:
* **Grievance Redressal Officer**: Data Protection Officer, MarketMaster Academy
* **Email**: `grievance@marketmaster.academy` / `privacy@marketmaster.academy`
* **Response Timeline**: All privacy inquiries and grievances addressed within 7 business days.
